WHY I'M NOT REALLY AN AGNOSTIC

This "not real graph" is another example of qualifiers but with slightly higher stakes. It attempts to illustrate how increasing the number of specifications for what you believe created the universe makes that thing increasingly unlikely to be correct...

While you're always technically "right" if you're agnostic (all outcomes are consistent with agnosticism) you're also not really ever making an assertion of truth, so, it's a very empty form of being right. I find it vastly more likely that atheism is correct than any particular religion because of the required qualifiers of religions. There are just so many more possibilities for the origin of the universe than a highly specific intelligent god, and correspondingly the diagram shows the possibility space that is consistent with atheism is huge in comparison.

POLITICAL DRIFT & THE ELECTORAL COLLEGE

The political shift right in the US seems to me at least to be at odds with a general progressive trend globally, though now it seems we are seeing US politics actually shifting global trends, which is worrying. This "not real graph" shows the shift over the past several (first term) elections.

I've estimated the magnitude of the left/right shift (the size of their bar) based on my subjective impression of the candidates.

I think this right shift is a result of the electoral college skewing results. If we look at the results had the popular vote determined the outcome, we see the opposite trajectory, one that mirrors the global trend.

This not only has an effect on election results individually, but election results have a real impact on what people perceive as the "centre", and so this artificial skewing of this political direction has had a real effect on actual political opinion, as we see with the final popular vote win in the last election. We have seen this US right-turn even influencing global authoritarianism.

MY UNDERSTANDING OF LOCAL MINIMA & MAXIMA

This is a sketch in lieu of a post—the concept of local minima and maxima is a mental model I often use to understand inefficiencies in the world. The basic idea is that many solutions to a problem aren't actually satisficing, but people fail to deviate from those solutions because, in order to travel to a satisficing solution, they have to traverse negative terrain.

A good example would be the transfer from fossil fuels to renewables. Before getting to the more satisficing solution, you have to bear the costs associated with R&D, social inertia, early inefficiencies, regulations etc. And so the development of renewables has been slower than necessary due to a reluctance to traverse that terrain.

MY UNDERSTANDING OF DOPAMINURGIC VS ADRENAL RESPONSE

This was one that I sketched out based on what I'd learned about the different physiological responses one has to adrenaline and dopamine. I had previously thought that the responses were essentially synonymous. But apparently, while dopamine spikes make you less sensitive to dopamine, adrenaline spikes actually make you more responsive to adrenaline. Meaning that if you continually spike your adrenaline in order to enjoy the dopamine high that comes with it, you simultaneously make yourself more stressed and less motivated.

This has helped me make sense of my increased anxiety when I indulge the vices of driving fast, playing speed chess or arguing online.
